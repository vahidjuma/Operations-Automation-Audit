# Webhooks Configuration

## Make.com Scenario Connection
* **Softr Trigger Block:** Booking Form Button (Action: Send to Webhook).
* **Make.com Webhook URL:** `https://make.com`
* **Trigger Event:** On Form Submit.

## Payload Data Structure
When the webhook fires, the following data variables are mapped and sent from Softr to the Make.com scenario:
1. `Acreage` (Number field indicating size of the farm plot)
2. `Location` (Text field indicating farm location, e.g., Shirugu or Salgaa)
3. `Client_Contact` (Phone number / M-Pesa details)
4. `Status` (Defaults to "Pending Assignment")
5. 'Trip logs' (Fuel, odometer readings and any other other information regarding a trip)
6. 'Maintenance' (Maintenance costs,information and status)
7. 'Customers' ( Name,contacts and feedback)
